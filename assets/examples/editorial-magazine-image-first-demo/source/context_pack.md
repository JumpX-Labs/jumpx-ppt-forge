# Context Pack

**Style Preset**: editorial-magazine
**Output Mode**: image-first

## Project Goal

Show that image-first projects contain prompts, manifest, and visible slide images.

## Acceptance Criteria

- At least 4 pages have `image_requirement.needed: true`.
- `images/slide-NN.png` exists for each page.
- `prompts/` and `images_manifest.json` are present.
